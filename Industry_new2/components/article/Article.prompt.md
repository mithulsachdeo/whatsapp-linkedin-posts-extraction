Use for any reading page. Children are plain `<p>` elements; the first gets a red drop cap. Add <PullQuote> between paragraphs.
```jsx
<Article kicker="From the archive" headline="We killed our NPS survey" byline="By Tom Okafor" dateline="30 SEP 2026" aside={<ShareRecord />}>
  <p>Here is what we replaced it with…</p>
</Article>
```
