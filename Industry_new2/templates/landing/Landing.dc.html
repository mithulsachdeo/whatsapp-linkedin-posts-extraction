How it works

Three steps · about two minutes
I.

Export the chat

In WhatsApp, open the group, tap its name, then Export chat without media. You get one .txt or .zip.

II.

File it at the wire room

Links are pulled out and de-duplicated across both groups. Messages already filed are skipped.

III.

Members read the front page

Sort by latest or most shared, filter by who shared it, and search the archive.

What we keep

Links, names and dates. Nothing else.

The import reads each message, keeps the LinkedIn links, who sent them and when, and discards the rest of the chat. We don't track views or likes.

Byline pending
If a sender only appears as a phone number, their shares still count. An admin matches them to a member when they're ready.
